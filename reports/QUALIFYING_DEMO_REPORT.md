# Major qualifying demonstration — 2027

Two simulated seasons at the top of the world calendar (2026 warm-up, 2027 to week 30): 662,976 holes, every shot simulated, in 27 seconds. World: 2,970 golfers (450 Premier, 700 Challenger, 1,200 lower-tour pros, 300 club pros, 320 elite amateurs).

_Stand-ins until the season system exists: tour fields come from members in ranking order; the world ranking and points lists are simple versions computed from these simulated results; lower tours are not played; courses are the calibration courses._
