# Golf Legacy — World Course Library

**380 fictional courses** in 259 real cities across 57 countries, with **793 layouts**. Every layout rated by simulation (100 rounds per level, 64 s to build and rate). Venue checks over 10 seasons: **all passed**.

Courses are generated from the calendar: ten seasons are replayed and every event (and every site of a multi-site qualifier) needs a free course of the right standard in its host city that week. That sets the minimum; the rest of the 380 are extra clubs where golf is busiest.

Easier layouts for lower tours: every Championship and Standard course has a Club layout, and 80 of the 200 Standard courses also have Forward tees. The Club layout plays about 7% shorter with greens a foot slower and lighter rough; Forward plays about 14% shorter. Same holes, same hazards.
