## Where hidden values are still used

- **Training choices** (what to work on, and the planner's value of training vs playing) use each attribute's true room to its ceiling seen through a persistent personal misreading (wider for poor judges of their own game, narrowed by a coach). The misreading is fixed per golfer and attribute, so it does not converge on the truth over time; but it is anchored on the hidden ceiling.
- **Scouting** by coaches uses observed level, the typical growth for the player's age and their observed improvement over the last year, plus scouting noise — not hidden potential.
- **Development itself** (what a week actually does) uses the true ceilings, as it should.
