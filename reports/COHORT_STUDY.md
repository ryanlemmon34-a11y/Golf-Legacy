# Golf Legacy — Cohort Study

3 independent worlds (seeds 2027, 3031, 4049), 25 seasons each, development on, every shot simulated (214,539,355 holes in 294 minutes of simulation). Generated by `python tools/cohort_study.py`.

**How to read this.** The numbers describe the simulated world, which is a *selected sample* of competitive golfers (the world keeps every good golfer and a sample of weaker ones), so its rates are not population-wide rates for everyone who ever played. Golfers who were already professionals when a world began are left-censored and kept out of the debut cohort. "Debut cohort" = golfers who turned professional during the run early enough to be followed for at least 12 seasons. Distributions are shown as 10th percentile / median / 90th percentile. Different seeds disagreeing is information about how much luck and circumstance matter, not an error.
