# Legends of Golf Masters at Georgia National — event realism test

1,000 simulated editions, fixed 83-player field, 72 holes, cut top 50 and ties. Compared with real Masters history 1995-2024 (approximate, by world ranking at the time) and typical betting-market odds.

| Measure | Simulated | Real Masters (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Winning score to par | avg -10.9, middle 80% -15 to -7, range -21 to -2 | about -11 on average; usually -7 to -15, extremes +1 (2007) and -20 (2020) |
| Field scoring average (rounds 1-2) | 73.92 | about 72.5-74 per round depending on weather |
| 36-hole cut line | avg +5.1 | usually +2 to +4 (top 50 and ties) |
| Playoffs | 16% | roughly 1 edition in 6-8 |
| Winning margin (no playoff) | 3.0 | about 2 strokes on average |
| Best player in field wins | 9.3% | world No.1 won 5 of 30 (17%); betting markets usually price a typical No.1 at ~8-12% |
| Top-10 rated player wins | 49% | about 15 of 30 (50%) |
| Winner rated outside top 30 | 24% | 2 of 30 (7%); betting markets usually imply 20-30% for this group |
| Different champions per 30 editions | avg 20.2 | about 20-23 different champions per 30 editions |
| Amateurs making the cut (of 6) | avg 0.9 | usually 1-2 |
