# National prestige: fixed-input replay, R alone (before) and 2/3 Q + 1/3 R (rules 2026-11-14)

Inputs held fixed: every nation-season's archived actual and expected fame (A, E) and the fame on offer in its starts (P: each site's fame split evenly among its starters, ability ignored), recomputed from the archived finishes. Shares are the juniors' (starting share x multiplier, normalised). A re-simulated world would also change the results; this isolates the measure. Weights are provisional.
