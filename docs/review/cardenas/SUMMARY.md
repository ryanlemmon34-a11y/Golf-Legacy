## Definitions

- **Start:** he appears in a major's recorded finish. Majors he did not play are non-starts and are not counted.
- **Statuses:**
  - "finished": an official position;
  - MC: no position after playing the two cut rounds;
  - WD/DQ: no position, with fewer rounds than the cut (none occurred).
- **Win:** he is the champion, the one golfer recorded at position 1. A tie for first is decided by a playoff before positions are recorded; no major in the save has two golfers at position 1. Five of his 39 wins went to a playoff.
- **Top 5 / top 10:** official position ≤ 5 / ≤ 10, ties included.
- **Every rate** is out of starts.
