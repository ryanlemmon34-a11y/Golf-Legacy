# Standings locks, and when rights and cards take effect

Generated by `tools/standings_calendar.py` from the calendar (`golfsim/schedule.py`, `golfsim/calendar.py`) and the card rules (`golfsim/cards.py`); regenerate it after changing either. Weeks are the calendar's weeks 1-52; a season ends after week 52.

## How the engine uses standings

- **Money lists** accumulate as prize money is paid: an event counts for its own tour's list; a major for every Premier tour's list; every major and tier-1 (Premier) event for the **combined Premier list**. A list is final (locked) after the last event that counts for it.
- **Cards** are decided once, at the end of the season (after week 52, `cards.apply_cards`), from the final money lists, the season's Q-School finals and winners' exemptions. They take effect from **week 1 of the next season**: nobody changes tour during a season.
- **Premier Tour**: the top 200 of the combined Premier list keep a card; winners keep or earn one through their exemption (a major in the five seasons before, a Premier event in the two before); a non-member whose Premier money beats the last member to keep a card earns one.
- **Major places** from last season (champions, top finishers, final standings, the amateur award, amateur titles) are worked out at the end of that season (`qualification.secured_places`) and kept in the rights ledger for the target edition. Places decided by a ranking or points list published during the major's own season are decided only by the list published at the cut-off (below): a projection is never a place.

## Reconciled with the engine

- The weeks above are read from the calendar the engine plays; the card numbers from `golfsim/cards.py`.
- The classic calendar (`v1`) played the Asian Q-School final in week 2: its cards waited until the end of that season (51 weeks later). The reference calendar (`v2`) plays it in week 51, so its cards take effect a week later, like the other Q-Schools.
- In-season Premier playing rights (full or conditional status at entry) come from the cards held at the start of the season and winners' exemptions earned during it (an exemption counts from the week it is won).
