# Amateur sponsorship: proposed starting settings (for approval)

Status: **proposal, not implemented.** Nothing here changes the simulation until the owner approves it. The
guaranteed major-trip funding (every earned major place) is separate and does not wait for this decision.

## Evidence it is sized from (the reference world "Test 1 10.30", seasons 2074-2083)

- A full-time non-college amateur season costs about $6,200-6,900 (median), and ends about $3,000-5,000 down.
- A major trip costs $5,000 at home, $6,500 in the golfer's region and $9,000 abroad (entry and travel).
- The amateur top 150 in a season: about 10 in the top 10, 40 in 11-50, 100 in 51-150; roughly 40 % are
  college players (their school pays school events) and most of the rest play part time.
- Zero-start seasons among full-time non-college amateurs are concentrated among the **unranked** (8.5 %);
  ranked amateurs almost never have one. Sponsorship helps successful amateurs; it does not fix the bulk of
  zero-start seasons (see "Not addressed" below).

## Proposal

| | Band A | Band B | Band C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earned by (any one, observable only) | amateur ranking 1-10; or a marquee amateur title (USA Amateur, Britannia Amateur, the annual amateur award); or a top-25 finish in a major as an amateur | ranking 11-50; or an earned major place (incl. conditional); or a continental amateur title (European, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, Africa Amateur) or the USA Mid-Amateur | ranking 51-150; or scouting: recent results (the observed form the game's own decisions use, never hidden ratings or potential) among the best 150 amateurs |
| Season support, up to | $25,000 | $12,000 | $5,000 |
| Roughly covers | a full elite schedule with two trips abroad | a full season plus one trip abroad | about three quarters of a typical season |

- **What it is**: restricted expense support, in kind -- it pays golf costs only (entry fees, travel, a caddie's
  weekly fee) as they are incurred. No cash, no living costs, no prize money (amateurs still play for none).
  Unused support lapses at the season end: nothing to carry over or cash out.
- **Payment timing**: the season's budget opens in week 1, set from the amateur ranking published after the last
  week of the season before and that season's results. Each accepted entry draws from it first; it counts as
  money available when a golfer decides what they can afford. No advance is paid.
- **During the season**: a new Band A or B achievement raises the cap from the following week (the difference is
  added); support is never cut mid-season.
- **Renewal**: re-evaluated every winter. When the criteria lapse, support drops at most one band a season
  (A -> B -> C -> none), so one quiet year is not a cliff. It ends on turning professional (the existing
  professional contracts take over), on retiring, or when the golfer stops playing as an amateur.
- **No double funding**: a cost already paid by a school, club or the major-trip guarantee is never drawn again;
  the ledger records each payer.
- **Ledger**: recorded as its own line ("amateur sponsor support", in kind) beside the costs it paid, so income
  minus costs still equals the season's net.

## Expected outcomes (to be measured, not assumed)

- About 150 contracts a season in a full-population world (10 A, 40 B, 100 C); about $1.2 m a season of cover at
  most, an estimated $0.4-0.7 m actually drawn (college school trips and part-time schedules use less).
- Ranked amateurs rarely miss an event for money; a Harper-like case (2047 USA Amateur champion, unaffordable in
  2048) is Band A the next season.
- Professional conversion is not forced; staying amateur may become slightly more attractive for top amateurs.
  The planned funding comparison runs (to be proposed separately) would report it.

## Not addressed (needs its own decision later)

The 8.5 % zero-start seasons among unranked full-time amateurs need a different response: the roadmap's modest
work/part-time adjustment for persistently blocked adult amateurs. It is not part of this proposal.

## Decisions requested

1. Approve, or adjust, the bands, caps and the scouting route.
2. Approve the timing (week-1 budget, in-season upgrades only) and the renewal rule (one band a season).
3. Confirm that no cash is paid (in-kind expense support only).
